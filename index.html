1. Data Processing Purposes (RBI & DPDPA 2023) 4 Categories
• Statutory Authority: Mandated under RBI Circular RBI/2017-18/15 DBR.No.Leg.BC.78/09.07.005/2017-18 ("Limiting Liability of Customers in Unauthorized Electronic Banking Transactions"). Immediate intimations for electronic financial transactions are compulsory by law.
• Processing Scope: Transaction timestamp, account balance, transaction amount, counterparty details, and two-factor authentication (2FA) OTP tokens.
• Data Retention: Maintained for 8 years under Section 12 of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) for regulatory audit and dispute resolution.
• Revocation Status: Non-revocable while the bank account remains active. Cannot be disabled for customer financial protection.
• Processing Scope: Intimations regarding fixed/recurring deposit renewals, interest credit certificates, loan amortization repayment schedules, cheque clearances/returns under Section 138 of Negotiable Instruments Act, and customer service support tickets.
• Category of Data: Account number, deposit tenure, maturity amount, loan installment details, and branch operational intimations.
• Retention: Retained during the active customer relationship plus 3 years post account closure for banking compliance.
• Security Scope: Automated risk profiling, transaction velocity verification, unknown device/IP anomaly detection, and security alerts issued by Namco Bank SOC (Security Operations Center).
• Regulatory Reporting: In case of confirmed phishing attacks or card skimming incidents, data is coordinated with CERT-In and the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (NCRP) as per the RBI Cyber Security Framework.
• Customer Benefit: Enables immediate hotlisting/locking of debit cards or digital channels upon suspected unauthorized access.
• Commercial Scope: Marketing updates, special festive loan rates, pre-approved credit limits, co-branded schemes, and customer privilege offers.
• Regulatory Standards: Fully compliant with Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations, 2018 (TRAI TCCCPR) and Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA).
• Voluntary Nature: Strictly optional. Opting out will NOT affect your core banking facilities, deposits, interest rates, or branch customer service. Can be revoked at any time without charge.

• Category of Data Transferred: Only your registered mobile number and necessary alert dispatch payload. Zero banking credentials, account balances, PINs, OTP tokens, passwords, or financial identifiers are ever shared with third parties.
• Specific Processing Purpose: Verification of customer communication preferences, spam prevention, and transmission of legitimate bank alerts across authorized communication networks.
• Retention & Security Safeguard: Authorised third-party partners are contractually prohibited from retaining, profiling, or monetizing personal data, and must purge transit transmission logs promptly after delivery confirmation.

Your Statutory Rights under DPDPA 2023 & DPDP Rules 2025

As a Data Principal, the law guarantees you unequivocal control over your personal data. You can exercise any of the following statutory rights free of cost by contacting our Data Protection Officer:

1. Right to Access Information (Sec 11)

You have the right to obtain a summary of personal data being processed by Namco Bank, the processing activities undertaken, and identities of Data Processors or DLT partners with whom data has been shared.

2. Right to Correction & Updating (Sec 12)

You have the right to request correction of inaccurate, misleading, or incomplete personal data. KYC changes (address, mobile, email) can be updated through our branch or online verification.

3. Right to Erasure / Deletion (Sec 12)

You may request erasure of personal data that is no longer necessary for the specified purpose, unless mandatory retention is required under PMLA 2002, Income Tax Act, or RBI banking regulations.

4. Right to Withdraw Consent (Sec 6(4))

Where processing is based on consent, you have the right to withdraw consent at any time as easily as giving it. Opting out will stop commercial offers and non-mandatory notices without affecting your deposits or banking service.

Rule 14: Right of Nomination for Personal Data

Under Rule 14 of the DPDP Rules, 2025, you have the statutory right to nominate an individual who shall be authorized to exercise your data principal rights (such as data access or erasure) in the event of your death or incapacity.

Rule 3(b) & Section 28: Appeal / Complaint to the Data Protection Board of India

The Central Government has established the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) under Section 18 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 as an independent adjudicatory body for data principal protection.

When can you file a complaint to the Board?
  • Step 1 (Mandatory First Recourse): You must first lodge your grievance with Namco Bank's Data Protection Officer through Tab 4 or by email at dpo@namcobank.in.
  • Step 2 (Board Appeal): If you do not receive a resolution within the statutory 30-day turnaround period, or if you are dissatisfied with the resolution provided by the Bank, you have the right to lodge an appeal/complaint before the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Step 3 (Breach Reporting): In the event of a significant personal data breach affecting your privacy, both the Data Fiduciary and the affected Data Principal may report directly to the Board under Section 8(6) and Rule 7.
Official DPBI Portal: dpbi.gov.in (Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Govt. of India)
